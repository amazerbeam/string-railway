DLR-111 — v1 Buff Card List Review
====================================
Card taxonomy settled this session: three buckets —
  GOALS          — achieved over the course of a hand (bank, survive, apply-damage, etc.)
  APPLY-TO-CARD  — attached by the player to a specific card in hand at play time
  UTILITIES /
  CONSUMABLES    — one-shot, used immediately, no trigger condition

Cross-product rule (confirmed): every printed condition template generates one
card PER combination of (suit or rank, if applicable) x (each reward listed
under it). Apply-to-card and consumable templates are single generic cards —
not multiplied by suit/rank, since targeting/suit is chosen by the player at
play time or use time, not printed.

UTILITIES / CONSUMABLES — cards used immediately, one-shot, no trigger
====================================

1. Protect N damage
   Ship: YES. Tiers: 1 / 3 / 5 (bronze/silver/gold)
   Mechanic: single-use shield. Absorbs up to N on the NEXT hit taken, then
   is consumed regardless of whether the hit was fully absorbed. If incoming
   damage <= N, player takes 0 and the guard breaks. If incoming damage > N,
   player takes (incoming - N) and the guard breaks. (Note: CPU currently only
   deals 1 damage, so tiers above 1 are forward-looking.)

2. Force a legal card from the opponent
   Ship: YES (overrides DLR-111's original AC2 exclusion — see ticket update,
   2026-08-23. Reclassified from a repeatable buff reward to a one-shot
   consumable, which resolves the original costing concern by bounding it to
   a single use.)
   Tiers: SINGLE TIER ONLY — one card, one force, no bronze/silver/gold ladder.
   Mechanic: play before playing your own card; from the opponent's legal
   moves, choose which card they are forced to play. Hidden cards stay
   hidden — but a revealed skull card can be routed around by forcing a
   non-skull legal move instead.

3. Extra discard
   Ship: YES. Tiers: +1 / +2 / +3

4. Peek the draw pile
   Ship: YES. Tiers: 1 / 3 / 5 cards

5. Read a suit (the Spyglass)
   Ship: YES. ONE generic card — suit is picked by the player at use time,
   not printed.
   Tiers: rules out 1 / 2 / 3 cards of the chosen suit (narrows the
   candidate list rather than scaling a number — see design doc §5a).
   Worked example: player holds 3 Bells, opponent holds 2 more (already
   observed at 3, 4, 5 played) — 6 candidates remain (6b-11b). Tier 1 rules
   out 1 of the 6 at random, tier 2 rules out 2, tier 3 rules out 3.
