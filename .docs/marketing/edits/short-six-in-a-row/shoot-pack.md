# Shoot pack — Six in a row is thirty-six

**Slug:** `short-six-in-a-row` · **Target:** vertical 9:16 · **Captions:** yes, burned
**Length:** 25–35 seconds · **Media folder:** `D:\Recordings\StringsAndStations\short-six-in-a-row\`

The number-goes-up clip. This is the most repeatable format the game has — reshoot a new version
whenever a run does something ridiculous, and it never gets old.

---

## Capture

**One live play session, talking the whole way through** — the same session that produces the skull
clip. Do not capture silently and narrate later.

Play until a streak gets genuinely long, and talk through the push-or-cash-out decision *as you are
making it*, before you know the outcome. That live uncertainty is the clip; explaining it afterwards
is not the same thing and sounds it.

**Talk while you capture and call the keepers out loud** — "that's the one", "ignore that". Whisper
timestamps every word, and that spoken marker is how the editor finds the right span inside a long
session. Silent footage gives it nothing to search.

Capture in one unbroken take:

1. The two numbers on screen from early — the running total and the streak.
2. Each banked trick, with both numbers climbing.
3. At least one moment where you take the roll-over instead of cashing out. Ideally one where it
   looks like a bad idea.
4. The cash-out, and the damage landing on the opponent.

If a run dies mid-streak and you lose the lot, **keep that footage**. It is a better clip than the
successful one and it goes straight in the backlog.

## Wording that is fixed

- **"One bad trick takes all of it — and they take no damage at all."** The stakes only work if
  people understand the loss is total. There is no consolation payout; a bad trick pays the opponent
  nothing. Do not soften this or imply partial credit.
- The numbers are **total** and **roll** in the game, but call them "damage" and "how many in a row"
  on camera. The internal names mean nothing to a viewer and cost you a sentence explaining them.
- Say "the opponent". Do not use the code's name for them, do not name the suits, and do not mention
  a setting — most of the game's nouns are mid-rename and none of it is settled. Everything in this
  clip works fine described mechanically.

## Notes for the editor

- Track the two counters. If they are small on screen, zoom or push in on them for the climb — this
  clip lives or dies on whether the numbers are legible on a phone.
- The rhythm should accelerate: hold the early tricks, then cut faster as the streak builds. The
  edit should feel like the risk compounding.
- Land hard on the biggest number the run produced and hold it a full beat.
- Caption the arithmetic at least once — *"6 in a row × 6 damage = 36"* or whatever the run actually
  produced. Do not caption a number the footage does not show.
- Do not describe a banked trick as "winning" if it was a trick the player deliberately ducked; those
  bank too. Safest phrasing throughout is "banked" or "good trick".
