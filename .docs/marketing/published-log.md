# Published log

What actually went out, when, and how it did. This is the only evidence for what works — everything
in `content-plan.md` is running on priors until this file has a month of rows in it.

**Nothing has been published yet.** First row goes here after the first post.

## How to fill this in

Add a row the day it goes out, then update the numbers about a week later. Views are the least
interesting column; fill in the last two even when they are guesses.

| Date | Channel | Title / hook | Views (7d) | Watched to end | Drove people to another clip? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The column that matters is the last one. A clip that gets watched and then leads someone to a second
clip is building an audience; a clip that gets views and stops is an afternoon.

## Review cadence

After roughly four weeks of posting, read this file against `content-plan.md` and change the plan to
match the evidence — which formats landed, which subreddits sent real people, whether the long
devlogs are worth their edit time. Evidence here overrides anything written there.
