Fox, Woodcutter, Treasure

Layout and interaction for DLR-163. Placeholder data throughout — a fake hand, a fake Swap pile, a fake Quarry. Nothing here is real game state; it exists so the shape of each surface can be judged before src/ is touched.

The 3 — name a suit

Playing the Fox used to offer every card in your hand and take one away. It now offers three suits and a decline, and takes nothing. Click a suit: trump changes and the decree plate on the left stops being a card. Bells is in force, so choosing Bells is the same as declining — the button says so. Arrow keys move between the four choices, Escape backs out without playing the card.

The 5 — the Swap pile grows

Playing your Woodcutter adds one to both figures, for the rest of the fight, and is never refused for a full pile. The control has to print the cap for that to be readable at all — it prints "3 of 3" today only in the sense that it prints "3 left". Play the 5 a few times and watch where the addition lands.

Swap pile at 3 of 3.

What to check: the mark is a thickened border and a boxed count, not a colour flash, so it survives a greyscale screenshot and costs nothing under prefers-reduced-motion. It clears on the next commit rather than on a timer, so no cleanup is owed. Duration, easing and the exact wording of "3 of 3" are yours.

The Quarry's 5 — a skull arrives mid-hand

The Quarry's Woodcutter swaps a card, and the card it draws carries a skull 40% of the time. That card is face down, so there is nothing on the table to animate — the only place the player can see it is the readout that already posts the Quarry's skulled count per suit. Trigger it and watch the row.
