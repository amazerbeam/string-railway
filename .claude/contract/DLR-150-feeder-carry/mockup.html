What happened

What this mockup is standing in for
  • AC6, first half — the dashed green line, Banking for next hand, growing every time a Feeder fires on a Loss. It is deliberately excluded from Cashes for and If you’re hit first, because AC1 says this hand’s cash-out pays nothing from it.
  • AC6, second half — the solid brass line, Carried in from last hand, present for the whole hand rather than only at the first trick, so a player looking up mid-hand still sees where their opening bonus came from.
  • AC1 / AC2 — the four trick buttons are the four outcomes. Only Clean loss and Ate the skull route the Feeder into the carry; a dodge pays into the hand exactly as it does today.
  • AC3 — Deal the next hand moves the carry into the opening figure, where Apply Damage then spends it.
  • AC4 — Fight ends wipes it.

Every colour, glyph, border weight and word above is placeholder and the developer’s to change. The line positions are the thing being approved; the wording is not.
