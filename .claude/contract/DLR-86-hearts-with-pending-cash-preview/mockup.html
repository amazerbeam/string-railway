What to look for. Take three tricks in a row and watch the Quarry's last nine hearts dim and pulse — that is bank × multiplier sitting on the thing it will empty, clamped by the row's own length when the streak would overkill. Lose the trick and those same hearts break in place rather than being replaced by a second animation, while one of yours breaks too. “Carry on” clears the reveal and the cracks settle. Switch the Quarry total to 18 to judge whether the band still holds together, and toggle reduced motion to confirm all four states still read at rest.
