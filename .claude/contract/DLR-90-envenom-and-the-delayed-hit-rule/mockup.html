What to click, and what should happen

  • Shop: buy Envenom twice. Coins fall by 2 each time, Envenom held climbs, and the third attempt refuses with the ordinary NotEnoughCoins reason — there is no cap on charges held, which is a decision to confirm.
  • Fight, arming: tap the ⚗ plate once → selected; twice → armed, and the hint changes to “Pick a card in your hand to poison”. A third tap gives the charge back unspent. That is the Cheat's own three-tap shape — the cost to judge.
  • Fight, marking: while armed, every card becomes tappable — including the four greyed out as illegal to play. That is the point: the Swan you expect to lose with is exactly what you want to poison. Tap one and it takes the ⚗ badge.
  • Fight, AC5: with a card marked, press the Quarry takes the trick. The trick resolves, the marked card shows its badge in the well — and the readout shows no damage and the bank standing at 3 × 3 rather than cashed. That is the whole point of the item.
  • Fight, AC6: reset, mark again, press you take the trick. Bank climbs to 4 × 4 as an ordinary clean win, and pendingEnvenom is booked against you instead.
  • Fight, AC4: then press Deal the next hand. The 4 lands, the hearts drop, and pendingEnvenom clears. Note what is missing: nothing on screen says why — the hearts just start lower. That gap is a decision for you.
