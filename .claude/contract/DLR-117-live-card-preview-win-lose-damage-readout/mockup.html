DLR-117 — per-card win/lose damage readout

Placeholder data only — no engine, no real state. What this mockup settles is placement and density: a strip in flow beneath each card, inside a .wc-fan-slot wrapper, so nothing is added to the card face (all four corners and the centre are already taken) and no grid row is added to .wc-shell. W is the damage dealt to the Quarry if this card wins its trick; L is the damage you take if it loses. A leading ~ and italics mark an estimate — the Quarry's card is still face down, so the trick's skull and Timebomb state are not yet decided.

Vertical cost of the strip: one line at calc(var(--wc-card-w) * 0.20) plus a hairline gap, minus the ~3.2px of slack already inside .wc-fan's min-height — about 7-12px on the hand row. Nothing else on the shell moves.
