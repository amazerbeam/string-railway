What this mockup is standing in for

AC1 and AC10 — a spent card leaves the pile and does not return, but still fires on the trick it was spent on. The log records both halves; that second half is the spentThisTrick field the plan adds, and without it the card would silently pay nothing.

AC2 and AC3 — every AP cost, AP pool and AP-capacity readout is gone from the three surfaces, and the shop shelf closes over the removed row rather than leaving a gap.

It is not standing in for the visual identity of the real screens, which already exist and are not being restyled. Colours, spacing and type here are the mockup's own.
