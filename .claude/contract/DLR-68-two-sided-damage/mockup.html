The Standing track

Replaces the top bar's single Standing cell. The x-axis is trick count, so each bracket is as wide as its trick span; the height is the multiplier. The ramp and the cliff are shapes, not six numbers to compare. Each bracket is pipped once per trick it covers, so 0-3 and 10-13 still say which trick you are on even though the multiplier there never moves.
