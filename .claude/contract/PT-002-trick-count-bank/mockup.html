Why "Both cards banked" has to go: cards are no longer what is banked, so the sentence becomes false on the day this ships. The replacement wording is a placeholder — the thing that matters is that it stops naming cards.

The "+1 ×" button is not in this ticket. It is here to show that the two terms are separate quantities that merely start equal — press it mid-streak and only the right-hand number moves. That separation is what makes a one-time-use item buildable later, and it is why the readout is not computed from a single stored number.
